## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose to develop a Direct Farmer-to-Consumer Digital Platform that connects farmers directly with consumers and retailers, reducing the need for multiple intermediaries. Farmers can register on the platform, upload their agricultural products, set fair prices, and receive orders directly from buyers. Consumers can view available products, compare prices, and purchase fresh agricultural goods at affordable rates. The platform will include location-based product search, transparent pricing, online payments, and order tracking. It will help farmers increase their earnings, reduce unnecessary costs, and provide consumers with fresh products at reasonable prices, creating a more transparent and efficient agricultural supply chain.
